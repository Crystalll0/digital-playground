HOW TO PLAY

Today's menu

Slide the fry basket under anything golden. Dodge anything dark or on fire. Every round is 60 seconds and things fall faster as the clock runs down.

DIFFICULTY

Five ways to rush breakfast

Higher levels drop faster, send more hazards and give you a smaller basket. Hard and Hell add a side-to-side wobble. Custom lets you set each dial yourself.
