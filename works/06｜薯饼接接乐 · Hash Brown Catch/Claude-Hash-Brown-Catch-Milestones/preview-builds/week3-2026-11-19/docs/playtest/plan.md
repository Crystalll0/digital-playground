# Playtest plan

**When:** week of 16–19 Nov · **Who:** at least 3 people outside the team (classmates, friends, family) · **Run by:** LI Jiarui, with one teammate taking notes.

## Script (about 6 minutes per person)
1. Open the game on their own device if possible. Say only: "This is a short game. Try it." Do **not** explain controls.
2. Watch the first 30 seconds silently. Note how long until they move the basket, and whether they notice the hazards.
3. Let them play 2 rounds (any level). Then show the shop and let them buy something with test coins.
4. Ask the questions in [form.md](form.md).

## What we measure
- Time to first successful catch
- Did they understand which items to avoid without being told?
- Did they want to play another round? Why / why not?
- Did they notice the potato dog? Reaction?
- Bugs or confusing moments (exact words if possible)
