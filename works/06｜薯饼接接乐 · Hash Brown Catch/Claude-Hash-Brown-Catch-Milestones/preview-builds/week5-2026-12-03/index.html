HOW TO PLAY

Today's menu

Slide the fry basket under anything golden. Dodge anything dark or on fire. Every round is 60 seconds and things fall faster as the clock runs down.

Every point you score is banked as a coin when the round ends, and coins never reset. Save up for new scenes, flavours, sauces, ghosts and bombs.

The Evil Potato Dog

Roughly one hash brown in forty is not a hash brown. Halfway down it goes poof and turns into a small, unimpressed potato dog. Catch it for double points. Miss it and it trots off the counter, smirking.
