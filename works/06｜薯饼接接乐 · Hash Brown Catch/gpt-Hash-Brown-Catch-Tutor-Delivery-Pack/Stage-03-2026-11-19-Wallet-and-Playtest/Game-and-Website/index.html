WELCOME TO THE CLUB

LET'S SAVE
SOME POTATOES.

  1. Pick a rush. Easy is a gentle start. Custom lets you set your own pace.
  2. Move your basket. Drag anywhere in the game, use your finger, or press ← / → (A / D work too).
  3. Catch golden hash browns: +10. Three stay in your basket; extras stack on a serving plate with a running total. A missed golden bite costs 2 points.
  4. Dodge burnt bites and chili bombs. Catching them costs 10 or 20 points. Letting them fall is free.
  5. Keep going until time's up. Space or P pauses; the clock pauses too. Scores stay at zero or above.

Golden catches earn 2 permanent coins, saved immediately. Misses and hazards never deduct wallet coins. Unlock Strawberry Social for 120 coins, Ketchup Cutie for 60, or Minty Phantom for 60. Cosmetics change appearance only. Saves are specific to this browser and stage. No power-ups or rare pup are enabled.
