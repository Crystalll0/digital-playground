# Playtest Plan

Invite 3–5 non-team players if feasible; use IDs such as P01 and record device and date. Let them operate without coaching or assuming a positive outcome.

- Find Start and finish a round independently.
- Explain which objects to catch or avoid.
- Explain score versus coins.
- Earn enough coins, buy and equip one item, then reload. Label any demonstration account and do not treat its balance as player earnings.
- Ask whether they would play again and why.

LI coordinates recording; CHEN and MA help interpret findings and retest.
