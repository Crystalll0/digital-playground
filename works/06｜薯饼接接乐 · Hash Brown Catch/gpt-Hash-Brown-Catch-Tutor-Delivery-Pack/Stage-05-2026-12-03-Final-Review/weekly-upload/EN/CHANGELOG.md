# Changelog

These are prepared stage scopes. Add actual release dates, contributions and changes from real commits.

- Stage 1: A fixed, gentle 30-second interaction: move the basket; catch golden hash browns for +10; miss for −2; score never falls below zero. Includes pixel animation, synthesized sound, mash and ghost feedback, pause, results and replay. No hazards, difficulty selection, coins, shop, power-ups or rare pup. Existing basket and plate collection remains as visual feedback.
- Stage 2: Adds a complete default 60-second round, burnt bites (−10), bombs (−20), Easy / Medium / Hard / Hell / Custom, gradual acceleration and local best scores by difficulty. Custom rounds offer 30 / 60 / 90 / 120 seconds. No permanent coins, shop, power-ups or rare pup.
- Stage 3: Adds a permanent local wallet: each golden catch earns 2 coins; misses and hazards affect only the round score. The small shop offers Strawberry Social (120), Ketchup Cutie (60) and Minty Phantom (60), with the default bomb retained. Purchases equip automatically and survive reloads. No power-ups or rare pup.
- Stage 4: Adds clocks (+5 seconds each, capped at +15 per round), coffee (half-speed falls for 4 active seconds; repeat catches refresh duration) and cola (one non-stacking hazard shield). A pup visit is planned in about 12% of rounds, at most once; catching it doubles earlier and later coins from that round only, never the pre-existing wallet or score. Opens the complete cosmetic catalog.
- Stage 5: Retains Stage 04 and adds a concise visible rules and score/coin guide, a separate Delivery Guide, and R-to-replay with a visible results-screen hint. This is a prepared final-review build, subject to future testing and tutor feedback, rather than a claim of final acceptance.
